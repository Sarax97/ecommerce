About ShopNow

ShopNow is a student e-commerce project built for INT424 at Ajman University. We sell everyday tech products at honest prices, from laptops and phones to keyboards and monitors.

This site was built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and it will grow lab by lab into a full store with a database, accounts, cart, and checkout.
